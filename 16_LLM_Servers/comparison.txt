------------------------------------------------------------

Based on the provided context, the recommended diet for senior cats includes the following:

*   **Energy Requirements:** The Resting Energy Requirement (RER) should be multiplied by a factor of 10–20% (and in some cases up to 25%) to determine the Daily Energy Requirement (DER).
*   **Protein Levels:** Healthy senior cats should receive diets higher in protein to avoid loss of lean muscle mass. They should not be protein restricted; a diet with a minimum protein allowance of 30–45% dry matter is considered moderate protein and is recommended.
*   **Therapeutic Diets:** Prescription therapeutic diets are often indicated for conditions such as chronic kidney disease, obesity, hyperthyroidism, chronic enteropathies, and osteoarthritis.
*   **Specific Recommendations for Kidney Disease:** Cats with chronic kidney disease may benefit from prescription renal diets that feature restricted, high-quality protein and restricted phosphorus levels. For senior cats in early renal failure, a diet with highly bioavailable protein supplemented with fish oil, L-carnitine, antioxidants, and amino acids is beneficial.
*   **Feeding Strategy:** Dietary changes should be implemented at home in a separate container to avoid stress-related food aversions.
